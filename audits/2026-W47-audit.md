# Oh Betty Jaletti — Weekly UX/UI + SEO/AEO/GEO Audit
**2026-W47 (Sun 22 November 2026)** · previous audit: 2026-W41 (9 Oct) · gap: 6 weeks, no audit ran in W42–W46

## 0. Headline

**The store launched, sold $749 across 17 orders — and this week the funnel stopped.** Week of 16 Nov: 183 sessions, **0 orders**, 3 cart adds, bounce back up to 89.6%. Sessions are down 51% from the 2 Nov peak (372 → 183) and cart adds are down 86% (22 → 3).

Three things are working: page speed is genuinely good, the product-page template is well built, and the agentic/AI-commerce plumbing (UCP, `agents.md`, `llms.txt`) is fully live. The problems are **not** technical craft. They are **stale facts and misdirected attention**:

1. The homepage is still optimised for a product line that no longer exists ("Linen Book Covers & Literary Keepsakes").
2. `agents.md` / `llms.txt` — what AI shopping agents read as canonical — quotes **wrong prices on both bestsellers** and does not know the gloves exist.
3. The homepage takes the most traffic (411 sessions), bounces 89% of it and has converted **nobody**, while the perfume PDP quietly converts social traffic at 3.76%.
4. The Advent calendar has a **16-day runway** (order-by 8 Dec) and is currently unsellable.

Scope this week: full live-site access worked for the first time (**closes W41 item D-1**), so this audit includes rendered HTML, structured data, robots/sitemap, agent surfaces, measured Core Web Vitals, and Shopify analytics.

## 6. The structural finding

Every single item in section 2 except E-5 has the same root cause: **launching a product does not propagate its facts.** The gloves went live on 16 Nov and six surfaces still disagree about the catalogue — homepage copy ("two pieces"), `agents.md`, `llms.txt`, `llms-full.txt`, the product count in the collection, and the shipping promise. The W41 audit found the same class of defect (SEO copy describing products that didn't match). It will recur at every launch until there is a checklist.

**Recommendation (H-1):** a release checklist that must clear before any product goes ACTIVE —
1. price + SKU + inventory policy set, 2. 4+ images with alt text, 3. SEO title (<60) + meta description, 4. "Details" spec block, 5. added to collection, 6. homepage/collection counts updated, 7. **`agents.md` updated and re-dated**, 8. shipping promise matches the policy, 9. schema spot-checked.

This is the one change that stops next month's audit repeating this month's.

## 7. Watch list / future considerations

- **Organic search is effectively zero (0.5% of sessions).** Fixing E-1 is step one, but the store has three indexable products; the 15-page Worktable cluster plus the guides are the only realistic organic surface. Treat F-1 and G-2 as the organic strategy, not housekeeping.
- **The business currently rests on one Instagram-driven product.** 12 of 17 orders are one $43 perfume. That is a healthy 0-to-1 position (research r010: one problem, one promise, one hero product) but it is a single point of failure — the 16 Nov collapse to zero is what that looks like from the inside.
- **Edition II (The Reading Hour) has four drafted products and a waitlist.** Correctly parked. When it opens, the release checklist (H-1) matters most; also resolve the $103/$169 Chaperone contradiction first.
- **"The Waitlist (trust draft)" page** (unpublished, edited 20 Nov) suggests trust-copy work in progress. Worth finishing before December traffic, not after.
- **The Scented Letter** (free sample, 244 units, 6 claimed) is **ARCHIVED**. If that was deliberate, fine; if not, it is the cheapest list-building and threshold-bridging asset in the catalogue.
- **AI shopping surfaces** are live and permissive here. Once E-2 is fixed, the remaining work is a Merchant Center feed and keeping `agents.md` current — the infrastructure is already further along than most stores on Basic.
- **Basic plan** still limits checkout customisation; revisit only if bundles or gift options become necessary for the Advent.
