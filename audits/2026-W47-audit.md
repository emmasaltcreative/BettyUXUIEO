## Correction to B-3 — the "28 countries" finding was overstated

I reported that the store was configured to ship to 28 countries while its policy said US-only, and
flagged legal exposure. Having now read the markets and delivery profiles, that was wrong in the part
that mattered:

- **Markets: exactly one** — `United States`, `ACTIVE`, region `US`. Checkout is already gated to the US.
  Non-US customers cannot buy. The shipping policy and `llms.txt` are both correct.
- `shop.shipsToCountries` returns 28 because it unions **delivery zones**, not markets. The zones are
  leftover configuration: an active `International` zone with live USPS/DHL rates inside the default
  profile, a `Samples — posted free` profile covering 28 countries for an `ARCHIVED` product, and a
  `Codigle Combine Shipping` profile covering Rest of World.

**No customer-facing promise is broken, and there is no urgency.** It remains worth cleaning up: the
International zone would switch on silently if a second market were ever added, and `shipsToCountries` is
what feeds and shopping agents read. Downgraded from Critical-adjacent to housekeeping.

## A-6 (new, Critical) — the Reading Hour collection is a live open-preorder page

Escalated from C-9 once E-2 was answered. `/collections/the-reading-hour` is **live** and states an open
preorder in six places — eyebrow `Edition II · Preorder`, hero *"Preorder. Your number in the edition is
held the moment you order"*, section heading `Open for preorder`, the SEO title, the meta description, and
a full `How a preorder works here` terms block beginning *"I. You are charged today"*.

The product grid then renders **"Between editions · Nothing here at the moment."**, because The Chaperone
is `DRAFT`.

So a visitor is invited to preorder, told they will be charged today, and given nothing to order. A crawler
reads a page claiming an open preorder with zero offers. Given E-2, every one of those statements is false
today. This is now the most urgent item in the queue — ahead of A-1 — because it is a live payment promise
the house is not keeping, not merely a mis-description.

The preorder terms block should be **kept and moved to future tense**, not deleted; it is the strongest
trust writing on the site. Full before/after in `fixes-ready-to-paste.md` §1b.

## A-7 (new) — made-to-order needs a stated window

A consequence of E-3. If pieces are cut to demand, three existing promises are at risk: the policy's
"in-stock orders generally ship within 3–5 business days", the delivery method literally named
**"Express — packed the next business day"**, and the `handlingTime` of 3–5 days proposed for Organization
schema. `BackOrder` is the honest availability value and fits the house model, but it needs a real window
on the listing.

**Open question (E-8): what window can the house actually hold for a made-to-order piece?** Everything
else about A-5 and A-7 is mechanical once that number exists.
