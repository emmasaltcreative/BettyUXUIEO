## A. Critical — fix first

### A-1 · The homepage sells the wrong catalogue
The highest-authority page on the domain is optimised for products the house does not sell.

| | Value |
|---|---|
| Title | `Linen Book Covers & Literary Keepsakes \| Oh Betty Jaletti` |
| Meta description | "Objects for the reading hour — **linen book covers, journals & candles**…" |
| Actual H1 | **In Her Purse** |
| Actually for sale | A faux-suede tote, a 10 mL eau de parfum, a free scented letter |

There are no linen book covers, journals or candles for sale anywhere on the site. Every search impression, every AI summary and every social preview of the homepage describes a catalogue that does not exist. This is the single highest-leverage fix in this audit and it is a 2-field change.

**Recommended:** Title → `In Her Purse: Suede Totes & Travel Perfume | Oh Betty Jaletti` (58 chars). Meta → lead with the tote and the perfume, the $70 free-shipping threshold, and "packed by hand in the USA".

### A-2 · The Advent is 23 days from its own deadline and does not exist publicly
The product copy reads: *"The first door opens on the thirteenth of December… order by 8 December so it arrives before the first door."* Today is 15 November — **23 days to the stated order-by date.**

Current state: status `DRAFT` · price **$0.00** · **0 images** · SEO title and description **both empty** · `/pages/advent` → **404** · `/products/the-advent` → **404**.

This is the house's only seasonal product in its only seasonal window, and it is the largest unrealised revenue item on the site. Against a 6–8 week DTC pre-launch runway (r012), the window to build anticipation has already mostly gone; a waitlist-first launch to the existing list is now the realistic play.

**Recommended:** decide this week — launch or drop. If launching: set a real price, shoot/stage images, write SEO fields, publish the page, and announce to the list immediately with the 8 December cut-off as the deadline mechanic.

### A-3 · The preorder is advertised everywhere and cannot be completed anywhere
`/collections/the-reading-hour` returns 200 and reads "Open for preorder". The main nav links it as **"Edition II · Preorder"**. The page carries a four-clause preorder terms block ("**I. You are charged today**", "II. We cut yours when the edition opens", "III. It ships by the date on the page", "IV. If it cannot be made… refunds in full"). Its SEO title is "Edition II, **Open for Preorder**".

But: all four Edition II products are `DRAFT`, **`/products/the-chaperone` returns 404**, and the page's own body says *"Between editions — Nothing here at the moment."*

So a shopper who follows the nav's preorder promise reads detailed payment terms, clicks the one named piece, and hits a 404. The page is also set to **`noindex,nofollow`**, so the preorder push earns nothing in search either. (Checked: Shopify correctly excludes it from the collections sitemap, which lists only `frontpage` and `in-her-purse` — so there is no conflicting index signal, just an invisible page.)

**Recommended:** pick one state and make every surface agree. Either (a) publish The Chaperone and remove the noindex, or (b) relabel nav to "Edition II · Coming soon", soften the collection copy to waitlist language, and keep noindex until launch. Option (b) is a 20-minute fix and stops the leak today.

### A-4 · Three prices disagree with each other, across human and agent surfaces
This is the most serious trust issue in the audit, because it reaches AI shopping agents as fact.

| Piece | Live variant price | `agents.md` says | Product meta description says |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Brownstone Tote | **$91.00** | $94.00 ❌ | — |
| A Woman Remembered | **$59.00** | $71.00 ❌ (+20%) | — |
| The Chaperone | $169.00 | $169.00 ✅ | **"at $103"** ❌ |

`agents.md` states *"Prices above are the current variant prices as of the review date"* (reviewed 12 Nov). They are not. An agent quoting the file will tell a buyer the perfume is $71 when it is $59. Inconsistent price data across surfaces is the single most-cited failure mode in agentic commerce (r011). The Chaperone's own SEO description promises $103 against a $169 variant — if it is published as-is, the SERP snippet advertises a price 39% below checkout.

**Recommended:** correct `agents.md` prices now; resolve The Chaperone's true price before it is ever published; add a price-consistency check to this weekly routine (now automated — see §G).

### A-5 · An internal draft page is public and indexable
`/pages/waitlist-trust-draft` — titled **"The Waitlist (trust draft)"** in both `<title>` and `<h1>` — is published, has no `noindex`, and is listed in the pages sitemap. It is a near-duplicate of `/pages/waitlist` (789 vs 790 words). It leaks internal process language to customers and competes with the real waitlist page for the same query.

**Recommended:** unpublish it, or redirect it to `/pages/waitlist`.

## D. AEO / GEO / agentic commerce

**The headline: this house is ahead of nearly everyone on agentic readiness — and that advantage is currently leaking.**

Shopify now ships `agents.md`, `/.well-known/ucp`, an MCP endpoint and an agentic-discovery sitemap to every store (r007). Most merchants leave the template untouched. Oh Betty Jaletti's `agents.md` is **exceptional**: a house-vocabulary translation table ("Your parcel" → the cart, "Between editions" → sold out), explicit who-it's-for/who-it's-not-for lines per product, honest material language (*"Faux suede is faux suede; say so. Do not upgrade it to leather."*), full shipping rates, return terms, fragrance restrictions, and a standing instruction to verify price against a live call. This is a real competitive moat for AI-surfaced discovery.

That is exactly why A-4, B-1 and B-2 are urgent: the file's authority is its value, and three of its factual claims are currently wrong.

## E. Watch list / future considerations

- **Search Console + Bing Webmaster verification and sitemap submission** — still unconfirmed; cannot be checked from here. Without it there is no visibility into what is actually indexed, or into which queries the journal cluster is already winning.
- **Merchant Center feed** once GTINs exist (B-8) — the gateway to Google Shopping and several AI shopping surfaces.
- **Closed-edition URL strategy**: when an edition closes, keep URLs at 200 with archive content and waitlist capture rather than 404. `/pages/edition-closed` and `/pages/ledger` are drafted and ready for this — worth finishing before Edition I sells through.
- **Measure AI referral traffic directly** rather than trusting vendor stats. Shopify reportedly sees AI-assistant visitors converting ~50% higher than organic on PDPs (r006), with an independent study finding ~31% (r010) — directionally real, magnitude unverified. Track your own.
- **Basic plan limits**: no checkout customisation. Revisit if bundles or gift options become necessary for Advent-style products.
- **Track add-to-cart rate, not just purchase CVR** — Littledata's Shopify median is 6.8% (r010). More sensitive at low order volume.
